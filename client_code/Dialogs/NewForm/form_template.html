New form

You'll write it like a document: type a question and press Enter, or press / for every block.

Fillfox guesses each type: emails, dates, numbers, ratings, yes/no. Options go after a question in brackets (A / B) or on lines starting with -. A * makes it required. --- starts a new page.

Use the link people fill in (Send → link). The form must accept answers from anyone with the link. Questions, choices, required marks, scales, grids, sections and descriptions come across; images and videos don't. Answers stay in Google.
