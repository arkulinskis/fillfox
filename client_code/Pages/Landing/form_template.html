How it works

From blank page to answers in a few minutes.

Write it

Type a question and press Enter: Fillfox guesses the type. Or paste a list of questions, or start from one of 12 templates.

Share it

A link, a QR code, or an embed that resizes itself on your website. Pre-fill answers through the link.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free and there is no paid plan. Uploads are limited to 10 MB a file, and gentle rate limits stop spam bots.

What's the catch?

There are no ads and we never sell data. Every form shows a small Fillfox footer with “never submit passwords” and a report link: that's how a free form host stays clear of phishing.

Who can see the answers?

Only you and the teammates you add. The people answering don't need an account, and Fillfox doesn't store their IP address.

Can I put a form on my website?

Yes. Copy the embed code from Share. The form resizes itself to fit, and works inside any page.

Does it work in French?

Forms can speak English, French, Spanish, German, Portuguese or Latvian: buttons, messages and errors follow the language you pick.

Can I take my data with me?

Any time: answers as CSV, the form as JSON, and a webhook for every new answer.
